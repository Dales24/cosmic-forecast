Help & Guide

Cosmic Forecast tracks and forecasts space-object trajectories. Load a scene of real objects, explore them, and fast-forward their motion.

01Load a scene

On the map, enter an optional Right Ascension and Declination plus a date & time (defaults to now), then click Load Scene. Real objects are pulled live from CelesTrak, NASA JPL, and SIMBAD and placed on the map. Use Debris field to show a named collision field or the deduplicated union of all supported fields.

02Navigate

  • Drag to pan around the map.
  • Scroll to zoom in and out.
  • Objects stay frozen until you press Play.

03Filter by type

Click any row in the legend to isolate that kind of object (satellites, galaxies, planets, and more). Click All Objects to reset.

04Inspect an object

Click any object — including the Sun, Earth, Moon, and planets — to open its detail panel, which shows:

  • Its real name, data source, velocity, and orbital coordinates.
  • A CNN classification with a confidence score.
  • An About fact and a Further info link.

05Play the simulation

Press ▶ Play Simulation to fast-forward at 200× real time. Objects advance along their real velocities, with dashed machine-learning-predicted forecast paths overlaid. The optional Cosmetic projected near-pass flashes setting adds visual bursts when moving marks overlap on the map. These flashes use the 2D display projection and are not collision or conjunction predictions.

06Classify an image

Use Classify Image Patch to drop in a telescope image and have the CNN predict what kind of object it is.

07Themes

Pick a decorative look from the Theme dropdown — Default, Purple Cosmic, Van Gogh, Tim Burton, or Pirate. Each restyles the whole interface and the sky; non-default motifs are visual decoration. When you're logged in, your choice is saved to your profile.

08Accounts & saved work

Log in from the top-right of the map to save simulation searches and classification results, and to keep your theme across devices. See them anytime on your Profile.
